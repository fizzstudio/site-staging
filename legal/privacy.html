Privacy policy

Fizz Studio takes privacy and security seriously. We collect as little information as we reasonably can, and we do not track people across our website, apps, or products.

This policy explains what information may be collected when you use our website, blog, online apps, or products such as ParaCharts.

Summary

In general:

  • We do not use advertising cookies.
  • We do not use third-party tracking cookies.
  • We do not sell personal information.
  • We do not track users across websites.
  • We do not collect analytics profiles about individual users.
  • Our online apps and ParaCharts may use local storage in your browser to save settings and preferences.
  • Our WordPress blog may use default WordPress cookies that support normal blog functionality.

Information we collect

We do not intentionally collect personal information from visitors simply because they browse our website.

We may receive personal information if you choose to provide it to us, such as when you email us, request information, participate in a pilot or study, enter into a customer or partner relationship, or otherwise communicate with Fizz Studio.

The information you provide may include your name, email address, company or organization, role, and the content of your message or request.

Cookies

We do not use cookies for advertising, behavioral tracking, retargeting, or cross-site analytics.

Our WordPress blog may use the default cookies provided by WordPress. These cookies support ordinary blog functions, such as login sessions, preferences, comment functionality, spam prevention, or administrative features. We do not use those cookies to track you across other websites.

Local storage

Our online apps and ParaCharts may use browser local storage to save user settings and preferences on your device.

For example, local storage may be used to remember display preferences, accessibility preferences, interface settings, recently selected options, or other choices that make the product easier to use.

Local storage is stored in your own browser. It is not the same as a tracking cookie, and we do not use it to identify you, follow you across websites, or build a profile about you.

You can clear local storage through your browser settings. Doing so may reset saved preferences or app settings.

Analytics and tracking

We do not use tracking technologies to monitor individual users or build behavioral profiles.

If we review technical information about site or app usage, we use it only to understand reliability, performance, security, accessibility, and product quality. We do not use this information for advertising or cross-site tracking.

Security

We take reasonable steps to protect information we receive from loss, misuse, unauthorized access, disclosure, alteration, or destruction.

No website, app, or internet-connected service can guarantee perfect security. We try to limit the information we collect in the first place, because information we do not collect does not need to be stored, sold, shared, or secured.

Information sharing

We do not sell personal information.

We may share information only when necessary to operate our business, provide services, comply with legal obligations, protect rights or safety, or work with service providers who support our website, infrastructure, communications, or business operations.

When we use service providers, we expect them to handle information appropriately and only for the purposes needed to provide their services.

Research, pilots, and customer work

Some Fizz Studio projects may involve research, pilot programs, product evaluation, accessibility review, usability testing, or customer-specific work. In those cases, additional terms, consent forms, agreements, or privacy notices may apply.

If a separate agreement or notice applies, it will control for that specific activity.

Children

Our general website is not intended to collect personal information from children. If a Fizz Studio project involves students, schools, or minors, we will address the applicable privacy, consent, school, and research requirements for that specific project.

Your choices

You can contact us to ask questions about privacy or to request access, correction, or deletion of personal information you have provided to us.

You can also control cookies, local storage, and site data through your browser settings.

Contact

For privacy questions, contact: privacy@fizz.studio
